
1. 游戏简介(Game Synopsis) 

核心概念与故事背景：

《无法校准少女》是一款女同性恋向心理恐怖恋爱视觉小说

“在一切都在往下坠的时候，那个双手可及的脸颊微红的少女，到底是浮木还是更残酷的深渊。”

你将扮演主角，一个能看到■■■的高二女生友子。在她的世界逐渐崩塌腐烂的时候，遇到了四个性格完全不同的女孩。然后根据玩家在剧情里选择靠近谁、信任谁、拥抱谁，会走向完全不同的结局。



核心玩法与体验：

本作以第一人称叙述推进，是一款恋爱冒险游戏，核心机制包括：

选择分支

多结局

好感度系统

4 位可攻略角色，■ 位隐藏可攻略角色

9 个结局，■ 个隐藏结局

1. Game Synopsis

Concept & Setting:

Girls Out Of Orbit is a yuri psychological horror romance visual novel.

“When everything is falling apart, that blushing girl within arm's reach—is she a lifeline, or a deeper abyss?”

You play as Tomoko, a second-year high school girl who can see ■■■. As her world gradually crumbles and rots around her, she encounters four girls with completely different personalities. Depending on your choices in the story—whom you approach, whom you trust, whom you embrace—you will reach entirely different endings.


Gameplay & Experience:

This is a first-person narrative-driven romance adventure game featuring key mechanics such as branching paths, multiple endings, and an affection/relationship tracking system.

4 romanceable characters,
